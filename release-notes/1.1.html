What's New in Version 1.1

  • DDC support for external monitors: brightness and contrast controls over DDC/CI.
  • USB hot-plug detection — displays reconnect cleanly when monitors are plugged or unplugged.
  • Disabled-display state now persists across launches and reconnections.
  • More reliable keyboard backlight restoration with retry on transient failures.
  • Better handling of synthetic display IDs: re-fetches after EDID stabilizes.
